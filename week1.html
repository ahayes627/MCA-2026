Week 1: Basics of Music Data

Task 1: Identify a theme

I've chosen to base my dataset around the American multi-hyphenate - singer, songwriter, composer, pianist, and civil rights activist - Nina Simone. Although referred to as the 'High Priestess of Soul,' Simone's music is difficult to pin down to just one style, switching between genres like jazz, blues, and classical. The expansiveness and diversity of her catalogue is both why I'm a fan of Nina Simone's work and why I picked her as the theme for the dataset, as I am curious about analysing the differences between different genres and instruments in both notated and audio music.
