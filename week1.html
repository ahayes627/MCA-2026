Week 1: Basics of Music Data

Task 1: Identify a theme

I've chosen to base my dataset around the American multi-hyphenate - singer, songwriter, composer, pianist, and civil rights activist - Nina Simone. Although referred to as the 'High Priestess of Soul,' Simone's music cut across many different styles, including jazz, blues, and classical. The expansiveness and diversity of her catalogue is both why I'm a fan of Nina Simone's work and why I picked her as the theme for the dataset, as I am curious about analysing the differences between different genres and instruments in both notated and audio music.

Task 2: Reflection

Consider your own experience with music. What do you think are some challenges to working with music and music-related data? These could be related to issues like access, curation, distribution, etc. How does your selected theme display some of these challenges? Describe the current manifestations of data relating to your selected theme, discussing how it is presently curated (collected and gathered) and how it is presented, described, and analysed. What strengths and weaknesses do you observe about these approaches?
